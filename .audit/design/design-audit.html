02

The design spine — 8 cross-cutting patterns

The brand is strong where it's committed (.pcard, team cards, dark heroes) and drifts where speed took over. These repeat across many pages, so fixing them once lifts the whole app.

01

Two shadow idiomsbrand

Core is the hard N Npx 0 0 ink offset. Soft blurred shadows leak into About's glass stat cards, the BlogPost graphic button and TeamManagement's modals (with backdrop-filter blur). Could be: one hard-offset scale everywhere; blur only inside the paradox sub-app.

02

Stickers globally flattenedbrand

v6.css strips border + box-shadow off every .sticker with !important — so the signature "hard-bordered sticker badge" reads as a plain rotated pill. Could be: restore the 2px ink border + offset on stickers; that one rule re-sharpens dozens of surfaces.

03

Four empty-state voicessystem

Shared EmptyState/EmptyLedger vs a lone 📭 (Notifications) vs ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ (Profile) vs a plain card (Teams). Could be: one <EmptyState> with the handwritten-Caveat + doodle voice the desk already ships, category-aware ("no welfare posts yet").

04

Three skeletons + "loading…" textsystem

Shared Skeleton, local SkeletonCard, inline .v6-skeleton — and tab-switches on Profile / TeamDetail fall back to bare "loading…" text. Could be: every async surface uses one skeleton whose block heights match the real rows (no layout jump).

05

Duplicated dherodrift

The dark hero is hand-inlined in PublicProjects + Opportunities and echoed in About/Collab — already diverging in accent and stat rows. Could be: one <DarkHero> component, props for title/accent/stats.

06

Two greens, sprawling radiibrand

Headline doodles use --welfare #1B8A5A; stickers/marquees use mint #00E5A0 — reads as two brand greens. Card radii run 10/12/14/16/18/20/22. Could be: pick one green per role; collapse to a 3-step radius scale.

07

Keyboard-inaccessible controlsa11y

Notification rows navigate via window.location.href; feed cards, team-card articles, ProjectManager <tr> and pending masonry cards are click-only divs. Could be: real <button>/<Link>, or role+tabIndex+Enter/Space (some done in this pass).

08

Energy stuck in dark panelsbrand

Brand personality concentrates in colored heroes and dark side-cards; the actual reading surfaces — PostPage body, TeamDetail tab panels, MyPosts cards — are comparatively flat. Could be: carry cap-bars, category tint and the odd sticker into the light content columns.

Directory & Opportunities

/projects · /opportunities
now
  • The strongest pages: .pcard (3px ink, № badge, dashed footer, ken-burns) + a sticky brutalist filter bar.
  • The dark dhero is inline-duplicated across both pages; the empty-state and general-application CTA are soft (radius, no hard shadow).
  • Mixed radii between pcard / filter / skeleton.
could be
  • Extract one <DarkHero> so the two pages can't drift.
  • Give the empty/CTA blocks the same 3px ink + hard offset as the pcards so nothing reads "soft".
  • Snap every card to a 16 / 18 radius pair.

About

/about
now
  • Bold dark hero (giant STUDENT / KOLKATA / NGO), welfare marquee, dashed timeline, principles grid.
  • The floating stat cards are iOS-glass — blur + translucent rgba borders — the one place the brutalism breaks.
  • Team cards were click-only divs.
could be
  • Swap glass stat cards for hard-ink rotated "sticker" stat cards (paper fill, 3px border, offset shadow).
  • Keep the scroll-transform hero, but let mobile inherit the same hard cards instead of flattening.
  • Team cards → keyboard-activatable (done in this pass).

Feed card & Post detail

FeedPostCard · /post/:id
now
  • Feed card is on-brand: 3px ink, cap-bar, resting tilt straightening on hover, particle Like burst.
  • But the whole <article> is a click target with nested stopPropagation, the comment cloud is a click-only div, and the sheet is inline-styled.
  • PostPage's light body column is generic; its dark side-card uses translucent chips (a softer language).
could be
  • Make the card a real link wrapper; lift the comment sheet into a class.
  • Give the reading column a cap-bar + one category-tinted pull-quote so it isn't the flat zone.
  • Trade translucent side-card chips for the ink-border chip vocabulary used elsewhere.

Notifications

/notifications
now
  • The flattest member surface: emoji type-marks, rows that navigate via window.location.href (full reload), a lone 📭 empty state and a hand-rolled error box.
could be
  • Rows become <Link>s (SPA nav, focusable); type-marks become category-hued .notif-mark discs.
  • Reuse the shared <EmptyState> + <ErrorState> so it matches Saved/MyPosts.

Teams & Team detail

/teams · /teams/:id
now
  • Team cards are excellent (3px ink, per-card tilt, emoji watermark) — but the whole article and its CTA fire the same nav (redundant, article not focusable).
  • TeamDetail's full-hue hero is the strongest brand moment; the tab panels below are generic cards with "loading…" text and an off-palette #e05c5c red.
could be
  • One click target (the card as a link); drop the duplicate CTA or make it a stop.
  • Panels inherit the hero's category hue as cap-bars; skeletons replace "loading…"; red → --tomato.

Auth — Login · Register · Pending

/login · /register · /pending
now
  • Shared shell is good (dark feature panel, dashed-tomato error with a rotated "!" stamp).
  • Register has no step indicator beyond a text sticker; Pending's masonry preview cards look like posts but do nothing; Rejected flashes blank while redirecting.
could be
  • Register: a 2-segment brutalist progress bar (filled ink / hatched).
  • Pending preview cards get a "preview" watermark so they don't read as dead links; Rejected shows a spinner, not a blank.

The HoD desk

/director/*
now
  • adminKit is a real 9-part system (AdminRow owns its own busy → the desk-wide freeze is structurally impossible). Approvals / PostMod / Achievements are the gold standard.
  • But 5 desks diverge: ProjectManager + VolunteerApplications run their own table systems (VolunteerApplications even hand-rolls selection); ContentManager is inline-styled with a tiny flat status select (no aria-label); category is drawn 4 different ways.
  • ProjectManager + ContentManager have no in-surface error/retry (toast only). StatusBadge/EmptyState are dead exports.
could be
  • Fold ContentManager onto AdminRow; retire the parallel vol-* selection for useRowSelection (bulk added here this pass).
  • One <CategoryTag>; add AdminErrorState to the two desks missing it; delete the dead exports.
  • Keep "chrome brutalist, data legible": stamps & stickers on headers/empties, flat unrotated row text.
