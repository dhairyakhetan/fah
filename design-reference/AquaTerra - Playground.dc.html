Food distribution drive, Topsia

volunteers
32
location
Topsia, Kolkata
date
Mar 2026
meals served
1,200

Before sunrise, 32 volunteers loaded three vans with hot khichuri, bananas and clean water and drove into Topsia. By 9am, over 1,200 meals had been handed out — one plate, one conversation at a time.

What we did

The welfare team partnered with a local community kitchen to cook overnight. Volunteers split into distribution pods, each covering four lanes, so no household was missed and nobody had to walk far.

The impact

Beyond the meals, the drive mapped 40 new families for our ongoing education program. Three of them have since enrolled children in ShikshAQ's free tutoring.

why we started AquaTerra

KA
kanishk agarwal
co-founder · content

In 2021, we were a group chat and a camera. There was no plan — just a feeling that we could do something instead of scrolling past it. Five years later, that group chat is 1,200 people.

AquaTerra started because school taught us everything except how to actually change something. So we skipped the theory and went straight to the field: a dog-feeding drive that turned into a weekly ritual, a teaching workshop that turned into eight Sundarbans relief trips.

"we skipped the theory and went straight to the field."— kanishk agarwal, co-founder

No fees, no gatekeeping

Every kid who wanted in, got in. We built teams around what people were good at — filmmakers made films, coders built tools, organisers ran events. The only rule: show up and do the work.

Where we are now

450+ drives. 4,000 trees. 15,000 meals. A streetwear brand, a tuition platform, and a marketing agency — all run by teenagers, all funding the mission. This is just the start.

★ the story

AquaTerra launched on 11 june 2021. 16 students. covid lockdowns. nowhere to put the energy.

the first project was a relief trip to the Sundarbans. nobody really knew what they were doing. it worked anyway. that became the pattern.

four years later, AquaTerra is not a traditional NGO. it's a student ecosystem: impact work, a streetwear brand, a tuition-discovery platform, and a free marketing agency — all run by teenagers in Kolkata.

the core logic hasn't changed: students learn best when trusted with real work. not simulations. not worksheets. actual execution, with actual stakes.

★ questions

good to know.

everything people ask before they join.

is there any fee to join?+
no. AquaTerra is completely free, forever. no membership fees, no hidden costs — just your time and energy.
how old do i have to be?+
most members are 14–19 and in school or college. if you're motivated and want to contribute, apply — we look at drive, not age.
how much time does it take?+
as much or as little as you want. some volunteers show up for weekend drives; team leads put in a few hours a week. it's flexible.
do i get a certificate?+
yes — active members get certificates and letters of recommendation issued by our HR team, useful for college applications.
which team should i pick?+
pick what you're good at or want to learn: welfare (fieldwork), events, content (film/photo/social), labs (tech), or operations.

chapter 01
the values.

hands dirty, hearts full, youth united. that's not a slogan on a wall — it's the whole operating system.

ownership firstyou own execution, not just attendance.
real impact onlytime in → impact out. no busywork.
fun is mandatoryif it's boring, we fix it.
community is the pointthe friendships are the whole thing.
psst — nobody here has a boss. you have a crew.
page 01 / 06
chapter 02
how volunteering works.

it's drive-based & optional — show up for what fits your schedule. every opportunity is shared, voted on, and briefed through WhatsApp. no pressure, no minimum streak.

  • opportunities are posted as polls in the Community WhatsApp group
  • each poll lists date, time, location & nature of the drive
  • voting “Yes” adds you to a drive-specific group
  • all instructions & content are shared in that group
minimum 4–6 hrs/month. most active volunteers put in 10–15.
chapter 03
how we're organised.

to keep communication clear, AquaTerra runs on three WhatsApp communities. you move up as you get more involved.

  • Community AquaTerra — every registered volunteer. drive polls & announcements
  • Team AQ — volunteers active within departments. team coordination
  • Core AQ — Heads of Departments (HoDs) only. leadership group
everyone starts in Community AquaTerra — that's where the polls land.
growwith us
chapter 04
grow beyond showing up.

want ongoing roles and real responsibility? join a team. recruitment for select teams stays open 24/7 — and AquaTerra is more than welfare.

  • team inductions — apply anytime; we read every one
  • ROOTS — student-designed merch that funds our welfare work
  • ShikshAQ — a student-built EdTech platform for accessible education
  • you're credited for every project you contribute to
tech & content arms run remotely — open to volunteers outside kolkata.
chapter 05
the facts.

the questions everyone asks, answered straight. we're a DARPAN-certified, student-led NGO in kolkata — REG: AAFTT2300ME20251.

  • completely free — there's no volunteer fee, ever
  • no prior experience, skills or portfolio needed
  • a director reviews your application in 2–5 days
  • most on-ground drives run across south & central kolkata
we have limited spots — we look for people who want to do real work.
chapter 06 · the last page
the vibe.

that's the manual. the rest you'll learn by showing up. we're a group chat that got wildly out of hand — and there's room for you in it.

welcome to the chaos. we're glad you're here.
