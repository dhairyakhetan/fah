# Full audit — 2026-09-10

Visual, technical, responsive, interactive and workflow, across every route.

**How this was run.** A DOM probe over every route, driven in a real browser at
375 / 768 / 1280, measuring: horizontal overflow (and which element causes it),
control size against the 44px floor, accessible name on every control, heading
structure, images without `alt`, and empty/error states. Plus the four repo
gates and the Supabase advisors.

**What this audit is not.** Every signed-in and desk route below was exercised
through the `?dev=` preview, which fakes a role **client-side with no Supabase
session**. Those pages therefore render their empty or permission-denied states,
so their *layout* is audited and their *data and workflows are not*. Anything
needing a real session is marked NOT VERIFIED rather than passed. Saying
otherwise would be the kind of claim this audit exists to catch.

## Fixed during this audit

**The desk search box was 20px tall inside a 46px bar.** Measured `INPUT 299x20`
on thirteen desk tabs. `.adm-search` is a flex bar with `align-items: center` and
its input had no height, so more than half of what *looks* like the search field
did nothing and a tap near the top or bottom edge missed it. `align-self:
stretch` fixes it; re-measured at **44px in a 46px bar** on all thirteen. This is
the same defect, and the same fix, as `.mem-search input` on the public members
page earlier in the session, which is what made it recognisable.

## Findings still open

### F1 · The desk has no `h1` (15 routes) — moderate

Every `/director/*` tab reports `h1: 0`. `AdminTabHeader` renders the tab title
as `<h2>`, and the only `h1` in the desk is on `/director` itself. A screen
reader user tabbing into a desk gets a page whose top-level heading is missing,
and the heading order starts at 2. `/profile/me` and `/yearbook` have the same
gap.

Fix: promote the `AdminTabHeader` title to `h1` (it is the page title on a
routed page), or give the desk shell a visually-hidden `h1` per route.

### F2 · Members desk mobile view — from the walkthrough, confirmed

Called out as "honestly very horrible... painful to look at" and not yet
addressed. Layout probes pass (no overflow, no small targets), so this is a
composition problem rather than a broken one, and it needs a design pass rather
than a lint fix.

### F3 · Data emptiness is structural, not incidental

Not a rendering fault, but it is why several surfaces look broken:

- **All 558 drives are in the past.** Latest `workshop_date` is 2026-09-05, zero
  future-dated, zero with `scheduled_end`, zero with a `drive_lead_member_id`.
  Any "next drive" surface is impossible to fill from current data. The recipe
  file already admits this in its own header comment.
- **`drive_attendance` cannot be written by the people it was designed for.**
  The INSERT policy requires `welfare_projects.drive_lead_member_id =
  get_current_member_id()`, and that column is NULL on all 558 rows, so only the
  16 super-admin/HR accounts can record attendance.
- **103 of 243 people on the cross-departmental sheet have no account** — no
  email, name or phone match in `members`. Report at
  `scratchpad/roster-unmatched.csv`.
- Category moderation scopes nobody; `member_of_the_month`, `certificate_requests`,
  `yearbook_entries`, `sops` and `points_ledger` are all empty again after the
  simulated data was removed.

### F4 · The card catalogue is 4/30 wired — the redesign's own point

~30 designed card shapes exist; only 4 can render, because the feed only queries
`posts`. This is the "post variations" item from the walkthrough and the largest
single piece of unbuilt design in the app.

### F5 · Three approved artifacts were never built

Home greeting, feed smart-grid, and the footer were approved as design canvases
earlier in this session and never turned into code. The footer is one of the two
surfaces rejected outright in the walkthrough.

## Two false positives, recorded so they are not "fixed" later

**`/director/roles` appears to overflow 274px at 375px. It does not.**
`documentElement.scrollWidth` reports 649 while `body.scrollWidth` is 375, and
`window.scrollTo(500, 0)` leaves `scrollX` at **0** — the page cannot scroll
sideways. The 649 is an artifact of the wide permissions table inside its
`overflow-x: auto` container: a child of a clipping scroller still reports its
full layout box. The reliable test is `body.scrollWidth` plus an actual scroll
attempt, not `documentElement.scrollWidth`. **This calibrates the probe itself**,
which uses the latter and will false-positive on any page with a wide element in
a scroller.

**`/pending` appeared to overflow 18px.** Measured mid-redirect: the dev-preview
member is active, so `/pending` bounces to `/`, and the nav was still laid out at
its previous width when sampled. Five clean consecutive samples of the settled
page show 0.

Also corrected during the audit: the probe first reported unlabeled textareas on
`/contact` and `/collaborations`. It was only checking `aria-label`, `title` and
text content, not an associated `<label for>`. Both label correctly. The probe
was fixed before those became findings.

## What passed

- **Responsive.** Zero real horizontal overflow on all 50 routes at 375, and on
  the public set at 768 and 1280. Wide content (the permissions matrix, the home
  strips) scrolls inside its own container with `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`,
  so a sideways swipe never triggers browser back.
- **Interactive.** The nav drawer does the whole job: `role="dialog"`,
  `aria-modal`, `aria-expanded` tracking real state, scroll lock on `html` and
  `body` with save/restore, focus into the panel on open, Escape closing and
  returning focus to the trigger. 37 dialogs audited; the three that declared
  `aria-modal` with none of the behaviour were fixed earlier today.
- **Keyboard.** 20 consecutive tab stops on the home page, every one matching
  `:focus-visible` with a real 3px grape ring. Skip link present with a valid
  `#main-content` target on all 10 routes checked.
- **Forms.** All six exercised. `/contact` is the reference: empty submit flips
  `aria-invalid` on all three required fields, points each `aria-describedby` at
  its own error id, renders three `role="alert"` messages and moves focus to the
  first invalid field. The one gap (`BreakModal`) was fixed earlier today.
- **Console and network.** Zero errors, zero failed requests, zero unhandled
  rejections on a fresh homepage load in a clean tab.
- **Database.** 52 tables, no duplicate permissive policies anywhere, every
  foreign key covered. Feed 15.5ms, every desk query under 8ms under real RLS.
